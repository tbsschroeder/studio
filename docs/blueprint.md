# **App Name**: METRO Pirate Funnel

## Core Features:

- Funnel Visualization: Simulate the pirate funnel stages (Acquisition, Activation, Retention, Referral, Revenue) visually using cards or progress bars.
- Customer Persona Creation: Define different customer personas relevant to METRO AG's wholesale business (e.g., restaurant owner, small retailer, caterer).
- Customer Journey Simulation: Allow users to drag and drop customer personas into different stages of the pirate funnel to simulate customer movement.
- Bottleneck Analysis: Employ an AI "tool" to suggest potential bottlenecks in the funnel based on the distribution of customer personas.
- Recommendation Engine: Use AI to generate recommendations for improving customer movement through the funnel, tailored to each customer persona.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: METRO AG's corporate blue (#007AC7) for brand consistency.
- Secondary color: Light grey (#F0F0F0) for backgrounds and neutral elements.
- Accent: A vibrant yellow (#FFDA63) to highlight key actions and insights.
- Use clean, professional typography that aligns with METRO AG's branding.
- Incorporate simple, geometric icons to represent funnel stages and customer actions.
- Design a clear, intuitive drag-and-drop interface for moving customer personas through the funnel.
- Subtle transitions and animations to provide feedback when moving customers through the funnel.
